# Glossary — Dice Game

**User** — A person with an identity known to the system, able to authenticate.

**Player** — A User's participation in one specific Game. A Game has exactly two Players.

**Game** — One match between two Players, played until one Player wins or the Game is abandoned.

**Turn** — The period during which one Player may Roll or Hold. Ends on Hold or Bust.

**Roll** — Throwing two dice; the sum of both dice is added to the Round Score.

**Bust** — Rolling 6 & 6: the Round Score is lost and the Turn passes.

**Hold** — Ending one's Turn voluntarily: Round Score is banked into Global Score, Turn passes.

**Round Score** — Points accumulated during the current Turn; not yet safe.

**Global Score** — A Player's banked points in a Game.

**Winning Score** — The Global Score target fixed when a Game is created (default 100).

**Win** — A Player's Global Score reaches the Winning Score upon Hold.

**Abandon** — Ending a Game without a winner by starting a New Game.

**Win Count** — Number of Games a User has won.
